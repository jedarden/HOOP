## Why the `cfg_attr` derives were genuinely compiled

`ApproveProposalRequest` and `RejectProposalRequest` gate `ToSchema` behind
`#[cfg_attr(feature = "openapi", ...)]`. That feature is **on by default** in
`hoop-daemon/Cargo.toml` (`default = ["openapi"]`, line 7), so a plain
`cargo check` *does* expand the derives — they were type-checked, not cfg'd out.

I deliberately left the api_reflection_ledger structs in the gated form rather
than making them unconditional. Commit `89214ef fix(bf-4ibg8): Standardize
ToSchema derive pattern in api_reflection_ledger.rs` intentionally settled on
the `cfg_attr(feature = "openapi", ...)` pattern; converting them to
unconditional derives would revert that deliberate standardization for no
functional gain (the feature is default-on).
